Titan BOS/Front Desk/Privacy and consent

FRONT DESK · PRIVACY

Front Desk privacy and consent.

Bookings and customer details stay in your WordPress database. Anything that leaves your site needs you to turn it on first. Checked against released version 3.0.128.

ON YOUR SITE

What stays on your site.

  • Bookings, customer details and settings are stored in your WordPress database.
  • The plugin does not create an account, collect a licence key or report usage to the author.
  • The IP address of each booking request is stored by default. To stop this, turn off Store user's IP address under Settings, Booking rules.

ERASURE

Customer data and erasure.

The WordPress erase tool deletes the booking records that match the customer's email address, and removes their client-portal sign-in links and sessions. Sign-in links expire after 20 minutes, and sessions last 2 hours.

LEAVING THE SITE

What can leave your site, only when you turn it on.

  • SMS gateway for missed-call text-backs: you choose the gateway. It receives the caller's phone number and one text with the booking link.
  • Webhook: an HTTPS address you enter receives signed booking events.
  • Twilio SMS: off by default. It is enabled only in wp-config.php and sends approved texts to recipients who have consented.
  • Hosted AI: off until you connect it and allow it. Before any external AI service receives site or visitor data, the site owner must enable the connection and review the disclosed data categories and terms.

CONSENT AND OPT-OUT

Consent and opting out.

A caller who replies STOP is recorded and gets no more texts from the missed-call gateway. The WhatsApp and SMS buttons on a booking form only open the visitor's own app. This site does not contact WhatsApp.

Read the Titan Zero website privacy policy and the terms.

Checked against Scheduling & Enquiries Manager, released version 3.0.128 (the plugin readme stable tag). Price and status source: the upgrade catalogue, version 2026-11-18.
