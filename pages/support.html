ABOUT THIS WEBSITE
Your enquiry stays in your hands.
This static website does not submit the support form to a backend, load advertising trackers or embed third-party analytics. The calculators save their reusable business profile only in your browser. The quote builder and checklist do not submit or save their entered details.
A calculator handoff puts the result and selected business context in the Titan pricing or support page URL. The page uses that URL to fill in the details. The URL may stay in browser history and ordinary hosting logs. Do not put customer or confidential details in it. A handoff does not send an email or submit a form. You review the support draft and decide whether to send it.
The copy button uses your device clipboard only when you press it. When you open the prepared email and send it, your email provider and the recipient handle the message. The hosting provider controls standard server access logs.
This notice covers the marketing website only. It does not describe how the Titan BOS application or future integrations handle data. For questions about product privacy, access, deployment or service terms, email support@titanzero.io before you enable a service.
