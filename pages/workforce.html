AI ROLES IN TITAN BOS

AI roles with clear jobs.
Each role has set limits.

Titan BOS is the system. Its AI roles help with daily work: setting priorities, running set workflows and doing approved tasks.

01

Manager

Runs one part of the business and keeps priorities and problems in view.

02

Specialist

Handles one set workflow, such as booking, scheduling or billing.

03

Worker

Does one task at a time, within the permissions and rules you set.

Assist

02

Suggest

Titan watches. You decide.

Titan flags issues and suggests actions. You review each one.

03

Autonomous

Titan works within your rules.

Routine actions run on their own, within the limits you set.

04

Predictive + Autonomous
