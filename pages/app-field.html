Plan the job, send the right person and close it out with proof.

Who it is for: service businesses that send crews to customer sites. First step: set up one job or one quote so the office and the field team work from the same record.

THE JOB OF SORTED AT WORK

A booking is the beginning. Delivery is the job.

A plan only works if the person on site can follow it. SortedAt.Work links the assignment, the customer details and the job record, so there is less back-and-forth to find out what happened.

Start with SortedAt.Work on its own. Add other Titan BOS apps when the business is ready for them.

WHAT SORTED AT WORK HELPS YOU DO

Connect the plan, the people and the proof.

See the work before the day starts.

Bring appointments, work orders, recurring jobs and capacity into the planning conversation.

Connect dispatch to the team.

Coordinate assignments around the job, available people and configured requirements.

Give workers a useful job view.

Titan Go brings assigned work, relevant information and job progress into the field experience. Offline support depends on device and configuration.

Keep standards with the work.

Use Titan Standards, checklists and quality steps as part of the operational process, not as a separate pile of documents.

Record what actually happened.

Keep job notes, photos, signatures and completion information associated with the work they describe.

Close the loop with the office.

Send progress, exceptions and completion details back to the office, so billing and the next job start from accurate facts.

WITH FRONT DESK

How SortedAt.Work connects to Front Desk.

  • Front Desk Free: Takes bookings and enquiries from your WordPress site. Calendars, customer details and reminders are stored in your own database.
  • SortedAt.Work adds: Jobs, quotes and field operations for the team.
  • Typical workflow: A Front Desk booking request becomes a job, then a quote goes out from SortedAt.Work once you connect the two.
  • Consent: nothing moves between Front Desk and SortedAt.Work until you connect them and agree to what each side shares. No connection is live yet.
