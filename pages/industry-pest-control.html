Pest control: plan treatments, materials and follow-ups

Plan pest treatments around travel, treatment time, materials and repeat visits.

What to plan for

Pest control mixes one-off treatments with follow-ups and recurring programs. The treatment, travel and preparation change both job time and materials.

  • Group work by treatment type. Include preparation, travel and close-out time.
  • Track chemicals and other materials as direct costs, using your current records.
  • Plan recurring program visits separately from one-off call-outs. Keep re-treatment assumptions visible.

The numbers below are starter examples only. They are not market averages, price advice or compliance advice. Replace them with your own records before you rely on a result.
