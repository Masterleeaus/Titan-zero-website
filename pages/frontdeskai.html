FRONT DESK · AI SERVICE

Titan Zero AI Assistant. A$29 per month.

The Assistant is an optional hosted AI service for the free Front Desk plugin. It answers questions about your services and availability, and it guides customers to a booking that follows your calendar rules. It adds to the plugin. It does not replace it.

Install the free plugin firstCompare the Agent Team

In development and not yet available to buy. Ask support to tell you when it is ready. Prices are in Australian dollars and include GST.

WHAT IT DOES

Clear answers, and bookings that follow your rules.

Answers about your services

Answers customer questions about your services and availability, using the information you enter.

Guided booking

Helps a customer choose a service and a time. The booking then follows your calendar rules.

Works with the free plugin

Bookings, calendars and customers keep working if the Assistant is switched off.

You stay in control

The Assistant only does what the plugin allows. Hosted AI runs only after you connect it and allow it.

HOW IT FITS

One plugin. One optional AI service.

Install the Scheduling & Enquiries Manager plugin once. Then connect the Assistant to your Titan account from the plugin's AI Services screen.

  • Bookings, calendars and customers do not depend on the Assistant.
  • Hosted AI runs only after you connect it and allow it.
  • Text messages use your own Twilio account, not the Assistant.

FAQ

Questions about the Assistant.

Do I still need the free plugin?

Yes. The Assistant runs alongside the plugin, so install the plugin first.

What does it cost?

A$29 per month, including GST. It is a monthly subscription.

Does it send texts?

No. Text messages use your own Twilio account and are not part of the Assistant.

Is it included in the Agent Team?

Yes. The Agent Team includes the Assistant, so you only need one of the two.

When will it be available?

It is in development and not yet available to buy. Ask support to tell you when it is ready.
