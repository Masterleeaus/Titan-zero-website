YOUR ORDER MAY DIFFER

Your workflow may start with a returning customer, an existing booking or an unfinished job.

The example runs from ScheduledAt.Work to SuperchargedAt.Work to SortedAt.Work. Your business does not have to follow that order.

Start where work gets stuck. Keep enough detail with each job that the next person can move it forward.
