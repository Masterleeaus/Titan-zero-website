Scheduling & Enquiries: terms and conditions

These terms apply to the Scheduling & Enquiries WordPress plugin and to its optional AI Assistant and AI Agent Team upgrades. They sit alongside the general Titan Zero terms and the Fair Use Policy.

Draft. Pending legal review before launch. Last updated 18 November 2026.

1. The free plugin

Scheduling & Enquiries is free software licensed under the GNU General Public License version 2 (GPL-2.0-only). You may install, use and modify it under that licence.

Local features, including bookings, calendars, customers, reminders and reports, are free. They do not require an account, a licence key or a paid upgrade.

2. Optional AI upgrades

The AI Assistant and the AI Agent Team are hosted subscriptions. They activate inside the plugin and are not separate plugins.

  • AI Assistant: A$29 per month, including GST.
  • AI Agent Team: A$99 per month, including GST. It includes the AI Assistant, so you do not need to buy both.
  • Both are in development and are not yet available to buy. Prices may change with notice before you subscribe.

3. Subscriptions and cancellation

Subscriptions renew monthly until cancelled. You cancel in your Titan Zero account. Cancelling stops future charges from the next billing date.

When a subscription ends or is cancelled, the site returns to the free plugin and its free AI allowance. Local bookings and data are not deleted.

4. Your data and hosted AI

Booking and customer data is stored in your WordPress database. Hosted AI is off until you connect the service and allow it in the plugin. Only then is the information needed for a request sent to the hosted service.

You are responsible for the customer information you enter and for having the right to use it.

5. Text messages (Twilio)

Text-back and approved callback or escalation messages send through your own Twilio account. You create and pay for that account. Titan Zero does not carry your messages and is not responsible for Twilio charges, delivery or availability.

You must comply with the messaging and privacy laws that apply to you, including obtaining any consent required before you send a message. Twilio is the only SMS provider supported today.

6. Acceptable use

Use the plugin for lawful business purposes only. Do not use it to send unsolicited messages, to breach another person's privacy, or to interfere with the service. The Fair Use Policy applies to hosted AI usage.

7. AI output

AI drafts, suggestions and summaries are not professional advice. You decide what to send, quote, charge or book. Check AI output before you rely on it.

8. Availability and changes

Hosted services may be unavailable for maintenance or for reasons outside our control. We may change features with notice. Free plugin functions will not be removed behind a paywall.

9. Liability

To the extent the law allows, Titan Zero's liability for the free plugin is limited as set out in the GPL-2.0 licence, which provides the software without warranty. For the paid AI upgrades, liability is limited as set out in the Titan Zero terms. Consumer rights under the Australian Consumer Law cannot be excluded.
