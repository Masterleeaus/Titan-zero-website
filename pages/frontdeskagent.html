Answer customer enquiries, capture leads, check availability, arrange bookings and follow up with customers automatically, while your team focuses on delivering great service.

WHAT MAKES AGENT DIFFERENT

It completes the process, not only suggests it.

Agent works under permissions you configure, and completes business steps through the connected systems. For example, when a customer phones to arrange a service, Agent could qualify their needs, check availability, create an authorised reservation and send a confirmation.

The booking engine, not the language model, validates and commits every transaction.

EXAMPLE WORKFLOW

From enquiry to confirmed job.

Illustrative example. This shows the intended workflow, not a live recording.
  1. CUSTOMER

    Contacts the business

    By call, website chat or a supported messaging channel.

  3. BOOKING ENGINE

CONTROL AND GOVERNANCE

Agent is not unrestricted.

You choose its operating hours, allowed services, approved prices, booking permissions, communication rules and escalation contacts. You also set which actions need your approval.

Every completed action produces an auditable record: what was requested, which system carried it out, and what actually happened.
