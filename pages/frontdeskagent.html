FRONT DESK · AI SERVICE

Titan Zero AI Agent Team. A$99 per month.

A hosted team of specialist AI agents for your Scheduling & Enquiries Manager plugin. It handles enquiries, booking and calendar work, quote intake and approved follow-ups, and it asks for your approval where a decision needs one. It includes the Assistant.

Ask to be told when it is availableSee the Assistant

In development. Not yet available to buy. Prices are in Australian dollars and include GST.

THE TEAM

Specialist agents, with you in control.

Enquiries

Replies to enquiries on your site and through approved channels, using your service information.

Booking and calendar

Books, moves and cancels appointments under your calendar and capacity rules.

Quote intake

Collects job details so you can quote faster. You approve every quote before it goes out.

Follow-up and callbacks

Sends approved follow-ups and callback requests to customers who have agreed to them.

Supervisory approvals

Asks you before any action that needs a decision, and records what it did.

Includes the Assistant

Everything in the Assistant is included, so you do not need to buy both.

TEXT AND VOICE

Your own Twilio account for SMS and voice.

Live voice and SMS for the Agent Team use your own Twilio account, which you control and pay for. They are not yet live.

  • Twilio is the only SMS provider supported today. Other providers are planned.
  • The Agent Team does not send texts until your Twilio details are added and the feature is live.
  • Twilio bills you directly for voice and SMS. Those charges are separate from the Agent Team subscription.
  • Local booking features keep working without the Agent Team.

FAQ

Questions about the Agent Team.

Do I need the Assistant as well?

No. The Agent Team includes the Assistant experience.

Will it act without asking me?

No. It asks for your approval where a decision needs one, and it records what it did.

Does it replace my staff?

No. It handles routine enquiries and admin so your staff can focus on the work that needs a person.

When will it be available?

It is in development and not yet available to buy. Ask support to be told when it is.
