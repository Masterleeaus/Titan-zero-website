Free business calculator

Invoice collection calculator

Estimates how much money is tied up in invoices paid after their due date. You enter the monthly invoice count, the average invoice value, the share that runs beyond terms and the hours spent chasing payment. It measures cash tied up, not new revenue, so faster collection does not count as a new sale.

Your business context

Choose an industry starter profile. Assumptions are saved in this browser only; you can change every number.

We will show the calculation and assumptions used.

Use your own records.Monthly invoice count times average invoice value estimates monthly billings. Multiplying by the beyond-terms share estimates the value outside terms; the separate hours input makes follow-up workload visible. Values are illustrative starting points, not market averages or professional advice.

The optional handoff puts the result and business context in the destination URL for prefill. It may remain in browser history or ordinary hosting logs; do not include customer or confidential details.
