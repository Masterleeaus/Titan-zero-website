THE DETAILS STAY WITH THE WORK

A customer is one person,
not a new record in every app.

01

Each request, job and follow-up adds to the same customer history.

02

Each job keeps its details.

03

The next person sees what they need.

A VIEW FOR EACH ROLE

Each person sees
what their job needs.

Each app covers one part of the business. SortedAt.Work is the office and dispatch workspace. Titan Command, Hub and Go give owners, customers and workers their own view.

C

Owners & managers

Titan Command

See priorities, customer activity and open work in one view. Decide the next step without checking several tools.

F

SortedAt.Work

Plan schedules, assign jobs and track progress in SortedAt.Work. The office and the team on site work from the same job details.

H

Customers

Titan Hub

Customers see information about their own service. Requests, bookings and follow-up stay linked to the work.

G

Field workers

Titan Go

Workers see their assignment, job details and checklist. Notes and photos stay with the job for the office to review.
