Privacy policy

This policy explains how Titan Zero handles personal information on this website and in its WordPress plugins. Plugin data is controlled by the site owner who installs the plugin.

Draft. Pending legal review before launch. Last updated 18 November 2026.

1. Who we are

Titan Zero publishes the Titan BOS website and the Titan Zero WordPress plugins. Contact us at support@titanzero.io.

2. The website

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies on this website until you have agreed to them. Information you send through a contact or support form is used only to answer your enquiry.

3. The WordPress plugins

  • Booking, customer and reporting data is stored in the site owner's WordPress database.
  • Titan Zero does not receive that data unless the site owner connects a hosted AI service and allows it.
  • When a hosted AI service is connected and allowed, only the information needed for the request is sent to it.
  • Text messages are sent through the site owner's own Twilio account. Twilio handles them under its own terms.
  • The plugin does not create accounts and does not collect a licence key for the free plugin.

4. Hosted AI and third parties

Hosted AI services process requests only after the site owner connects and allows them. Third-party providers, such as Twilio, process data under their own privacy policies.

5. Storage and security

Data is stored in the site owner's database and in hosted services only where the owner has connected them. We take reasonable steps to protect information in our control.

6. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. Site owners control the plugin data stored in their own WordPress sites.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: support@titanzero.io.
