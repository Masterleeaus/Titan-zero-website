Electrical business tools and planning

Estimate electrical service capacity and job contribution using labour, materials, travel and test or close-out time.

What to include in the plan

Electrical service businesses need a job model that includes travel, material handling, scope changes and time for safe completion records. Compare the estimate with the actual work delivered.

  • Include preparation, travel and close-out or documentation time in job duration.
  • Separate labour and materials so a change in parts cost does not hide the work contribution.
  • Use qualified staff availability and job mix when comparing theoretical hours with bookable capacity.

The calculator values below are illustrative starter assumptions only. They are not market averages, price recommendations or compliance advice. Replace them with your own records before relying on a result.
