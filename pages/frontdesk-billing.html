FRONT DESK · SUPPORT

Billing and cancellation for Front Desk.

No billing is live for Front Desk. Front Desk Free has no subscription and no licence key, so there is nothing to cancel.

BILLING TODAY

What is billed today.

  • Front Desk Free: free. Nothing to pay and nothing to cancel.
  • Titan Zero AI Assistant, A$29 per month incl. GST: in development and not available to buy.
  • Titan Zero AI Agent Team, A$99 per month incl. GST: in development and not available to buy.
  • Connected apps: request a quote. No checkout is live.

CANCELLATION

How a hosted AI subscription would be cancelled.

The draft terms describe how a subscription would work once billing is live. The terms are drafts pending legal review.

  • Subscriptions renew monthly until cancelled.
  • You cancel in your Titan Zero account.
  • Cancelling stops future charges from the next billing date.
  • After cancelling, the site returns to the free plugin. Your bookings and settings are kept.

OTHER CHARGES

Charges that do not come from Titan Zero.

Text messages use your own Twilio account, which you control and pay for directly. Titan Zero does not bill you for Twilio.

Read the plugin terms, the Titan Zero terms and the Fair Use Policy.

Checked against Scheduling & Enquiries Manager, released version 3.0.128 (the plugin readme stable tag). Price and status source: the upgrade catalogue, version 2026-11-18.
