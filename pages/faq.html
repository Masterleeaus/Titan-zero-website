Scheduling & Enquiries: frequently asked questions

Getting started

What is Scheduling & Enquiries?

It is a free WordPress plugin for service businesses, such as plumbers, cleaners, salons, tutors and clinics. It takes bookings, keeps calendars, customers and reminders, and handles enquiries with built-in rules.

Is it free?

Yes. Bookings, calendars, customers, reminders, reports and rules-based enquiry handling are free. You do not need a licence key or an account.

What does my site need to run it?

Your site needs WordPress 6.6 or later and PHP 7.4 or later. The plugin's readme lists WordPress 7.1 as the latest tested version.

Does it need other Titan Zero plugins?

No. It runs on its own, without any other Titan Zero plugin.

Is it on WordPress.org yet?

Not yet. Email support@titanzero.io for the current download.

How do I set it up?

Install the plugin, then follow these steps.

  1. Install and activate the plugin.
  2. Open the setup wizard. Enter your business details, services, working hours and calendars.
  3. Publish your booking form on a page.
  4. In Settings, create the booking page and client portal.
Can I import bookings from Appointment Hour Booking or Titan Front Desk?

No, not automatically. This version has no import tool. Keep the old plugin until you have what you need, then enter any remaining bookings by hand.

Bookings and calendars

What can customers do on the booking form?

Customers pick a free time slot for a service. Booking rules check working hours, durations and capacity before the booking is saved.

Can I have more than one calendar or staff member?

Yes. For each calendar, set working hours, service durations and staff or resource capacity.

How do customers change or cancel a booking?

Customers change or cancel a booking in the client portal.

  1. Open the client portal.
  2. Sign in with your email address.
  3. View your upcoming bookings, then reschedule or cancel one.
How do I see my bookings?

Use the admin calendar or the appointment list. You can change a booking's status there, and export appointments to CSV.

Do reminders and follow-ups go out automatically?

Yes. The plugin sends email notifications, customer reminders and follow-ups for each booking. Reminders are re-queued after a customer reschedules or a status changes.

Does it include quotes or invoices?

No. Quotes and invoices are not part of the free plugin, and you do not need them to take bookings. Quotes & Pricing and Invoices & Payments are separate plugins.

Enquiries and industry packs

How are enquiries handled without AI?

Rules-based handling is part of the free plugin. It answers questions and routes enquiries using the rules you set.

What are industry packs?

Industry packs are example services and answers for a trade or service type. Each pack is a starting point, so review and edit every answer before you publish your form.

Can I write my own pack?

Yes. You can upload or register custom packs.

AI upgrades

Is the free plugin AI-powered?

The free plugin includes rules-based enquiry handling and a limited free AI allowance. Hosted AI runs only after you connect it and allow it in the plugin.

What is the AI Assistant?

The AI Assistant is a hosted conversational AI service for your site. It answers enquiries, guides customers to a booking, drafts replies and quotes, and writes owner summaries. Price: A$29 per month, including GST.

What is the AI Agent Team?

The AI Agent Team is a hosted group of specialist agents for enquiries, booking and calendar work, quote intake and approved follow-up. It includes the AI Assistant. Price: A$99 per month, including GST.

Do I need both?

No. The AI Agent Team includes the AI Assistant.

Will the agents act without asking me?

No. They work within the approvals you set, ask you before any decision that needs one, and record what they did.

Are the AI upgrades available now?

No. They are in development and not yet available to buy.

What happens if I cancel?

The site returns to the free plugin and its free AI allowance. Your bookings and settings are kept.

Text messages

Which SMS provider can I use?

Twilio, using your own account. Twilio is the only SMS provider supported today, and other providers are planned.

Who pays for texts?

You do. Texts go through your own Twilio account, and Twilio bills you directly.

Is SMS on by default?

No. SMS stays off until your site administrator adds your Twilio details.

What texts does the plugin send?

It sends text-backs to missed calls, and approved callback or escalation messages. Live voice and SMS for the Agent Team will use the same Twilio setup when they are available.

Pricing and billing

Is there a Pro plugin?

No. There is no Pro plugin or Pro licence. The free plugin is the whole plugin, and the AI upgrades are added inside it.

How are the AI upgrades billed?

The AI upgrades are monthly subscriptions in Australian dollars, including GST. Renewal and cancellation terms are shown before you subscribe. See the Terms.

Do Twilio charges come from Titan Zero?

No. Twilio bills you directly for your own account.

Data and privacy

Where is my data stored?

Your data is stored in your WordPress database. The free plugin needs no account and sends nothing to Titan Zero.

Does Titan Zero see my bookings or customers?

Not unless you connect a hosted AI service and allow it. Only then is the information needed for a request sent to that service.

Does the plugin store IP addresses?

Yes. The plugin stores the IP address of each booking request by default. You can turn this off under Booking rules.

What happens to my data if I uninstall?

Your bookings and settings are kept. Data is deleted only if you add the data-deletion line to wp-config.php before you delete the plugin. The plugin's own documentation describes that line.

How do I erase a customer's data?

Use WordPress's personal data erase tool in these three steps.

  1. Open the tool.
  2. Enter the customer's email address.
  3. Run the erase. It deletes the booking records that match that email address, and removes the customer's client-portal sign-in links and sessions.

Support and licence

How do I get help?

Follow these steps to get help.

  1. Email support@titanzero.io.
  2. Include your WordPress and PHP versions.
  3. Say what you expected to happen.
How do I report a security problem?

Email support@titanzero.io. Please do not post exploit details publicly.

What licence is it under?

It is licensed under GPL-2.0-only. The source is public, and you may use and modify it under that licence.

Where are the terms?

The plugin terms and conditions are at plugin terms and conditions. The Titan Zero terms and the Fair Use Policy also apply.
