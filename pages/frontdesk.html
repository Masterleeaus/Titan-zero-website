Titan BOS/Front Desk

FRONT DESK

Bookings that run on your WordPress site. Free to start.

Front Desk is a WordPress booking system for service businesses. Customers pick a time from your booking form, and you manage calendars, customers, reminders and reports from one place. The free plugin works without AI and without a licence key.

FRONT DESK FREE

What the free plugin does today.

Everything below is included in the free Titan Zero Front Desk plugin, and none of it needs a paid version or a licence key.

Booking form and calendars

Customers choose a free time slot from your form. Set working hours, service durations and staff or resource capacity.

Booking page and client portal

Create them from Settings. They use your theme's layout. Clients sign in with their email address to view, change or cancel upcoming bookings.

Customers and services

Keep local customer records, addresses and notes, and a service catalogue for your calendars.

Notifications and reminders

Email notifications, customer reminders and follow-ups, with optional reminders for administrators on Front Desk screens.

Admin calendar and reports

An admin calendar and appointment list, status changes, a dashboard widget with upcoming appointments and CSV export.

Setup guidance

A setup wizard for your details and services. Review and edit every answer before you publish your form.

Optional text-back to missed calls uses your own phone system and SMS gateway. Quotes and invoices are not part of the free plugin and are not needed for booking.

THE CUSTOMER JOURNEY

From enquiry to delivered job.

Front Desk covers the booking side of the job. It records who is booked, when and for what service, and keeps the calendar and customer details in one place.

  1. 1. Enquiry. A customer finds your booking form on your site and picks a time.
  2. 2. Booking. The booking is saved against the right calendar, with capacity checked and a confirmation email sent.
  3. 3. Reminders and changes. Reminders and follow-ups go out, and customers can reschedule or cancel from the client portal.
  4. 4. Delivery and records. You update status in the admin calendar and export appointments as CSV.

WHO IT IS FOR

Service businesses with bookings to manage.

Front Desk is built for service businesses such as plumbers, cleaners, salons, tutors and clinics. Optional industry packs supply example services and answers. Each one is a starting point, so check and change it before you publish your booking form.

SETUP

Install and set up in WordPress.

Front Desk is a new plugin, not an upgrade. It does not import from other booking plugins.

  1. 1. Install. Upload the titan-zero-trades-front-desk folder to /wp-content/plugins/, or install it from the Plugins screen.
  2. 2. Activate. Activate it on a supported WordPress site. No other Titan Zero plugin is needed.
  3. 3. Configure. Open Front Desk in the admin menu and enter your business details, services, calendars and working hours.
  4. 4. Publish. Publish your booking form on a page, then create the booking page and client portal from Settings.

PRIVACY AND DATA

Your data stays in your WordPress database.

Front Desk does not create an account, collect a licence key or report usage to the author. Bookings and customer details are stored in your database.

  • External services are used only when you turn them on: an SMS gateway you choose, a webhook you set up, a web page you name for service import, or Twilio if you enable it.
  • The IP address of each booking request is stored by default. You can turn this off under Booking rules.
  • Uninstalling keeps your bookings and settings unless you add the data-deletion constant described in the plugin's FAQ.
  • Front Desk works fully without any upgrade.

Full details are in the plugin's readme and the Terms.

FAQ

Common questions.

Does Front Desk need AI?

No. The free plugin works without AI. AI is a separate hosted product, Front Desk AI.

Do I need an account or licence key?

No. The free plugin needs no account and no licence key.

Can I move my data from Appointment Hour Booking or Titan Front Desk?

Not automatically. Front Desk has no import tool in this version, so keep the old plugin until you have what you need, then enter any remaining bookings by hand.

Is the free plugin locked behind a paid version?

No. The free plugin is GPL-2.0 licensed, and nothing in it is locked behind a paid version or licence key. Upgrade pricing is set on the pricing page before each upgrade launches.

Where do I get help?

Email support@titanzero.io. For security problems, email the same address and do not post exploit details publicly.
