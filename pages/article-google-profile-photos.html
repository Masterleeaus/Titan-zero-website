Practical guide

Post finished-job photos to your Google profile, with consent

A monthly routine for finished-job photos, with written consent before any customer, home or address can be shown.

Post one finished job each month

Choose one finished job each month and photograph the completed work. Keep the caption short and accurate: what was done, and nothing about the price or the customer.

Post on the same day each month if you can. A regular rhythm is easier to keep than a burst of photos.

Leave these out unless the customer agrees in writing

Faces, including the customer's, their family's and their pets' where a person can be recognised.

House numbers, street names and anything else that shows the address.

Name tags, letters, number plates and anything else that identifies the customer.

Before you post any photo

Check the photo with the job record. If a customer, their home or their property can be identified, get written consent first using the form below.

If in doubt, leave the photo out or crop it to the work itself.

Customer photo consent form

Use this form, or a copy of it, before any photo of a customer, their home or their property is posted. Keep the signed copy with the job record. Consent is optional. A customer who says no will not be affected in any way.

Pending legal review: this form and the privacy wording below have not been reviewed by a lawyer. Do not use them with customers until that review is complete.

When a customer asks you to remove a photo

Remove the photo from your Google Business Profile and from your website as soon as you can, and within five working days at the latest.

Record the request, the date and the photo removed, so the request can be shown to be dealt with.

Confirm to the customer, in a short message, that the photo has been removed. Do not argue about it or ask for a reason.

Withdrawing consent stops future use of the photo. It does not change any service already delivered.

Privacy wording

We use your photos and job details only for the purposes you agree to on this form. Where we use your photos to promote our business, the direct marketing rules in Australian Privacy Principle 7 (APP 7) under the Privacy Act 1988 apply. We will not use your personal information to market to you without your consent.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting [business name] on [phone or email]. We will respect your request and stop further use of the photos.
