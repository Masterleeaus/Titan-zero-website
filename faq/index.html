FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Questions about the Titan Zero plugins, the AI upgrades, the app upgrades and our terms.

Answers are general information. The terms and privacy policy are drafts pending legal review.

Plugins

What is Scheduling & Enquiries?

A free WordPress plugin for bookings, calendars, customers, reminders and enquiries, for service businesses. See the Front Desk overview.

Is it free?

Yes. Local features are free and do not need a licence key. Optional AI upgrades are paid subscriptions.

Is there a Pro plugin?

No. Every plugin is free. Optional AI upgrades and app upgrades are added inside the plugin.

Where is the full plugin list?

On the plugins page.

AI upgrades

What are the AI upgrades?

The AI Assistant helps with enquiries, drafts and suggestions. The AI Agent Team runs specialist agents for approved work and includes the Assistant. Both are hosted subscriptions inside the plugin.

What do they cost?

For Scheduling & Enquiries, the AI Assistant is A$29 per month and the AI Agent Team is A$99 per month, including GST. Other plugins are priced by how much AI work they do. See the plugins page.

Are they available now?

No. They are in development and not yet available to buy.

Do I need to buy both?

No. The AI Agent Team includes the Assistant.

App upgrades

Which apps are available?

SuperchargedAt.Work (AI workforce), SkilledAt.Work (training), SortedAt.Work (field and operations) and ScheduledAt.Work (bookings, websites and landing pages). See the app upgrades.

Do the apps work with every plugin?

Yes. Each app is available with every Titan Zero plugin, and you can add any of them on its own.

Text messages

Which SMS provider do I need?

Twilio, using your own account. Twilio is the only SMS provider supported today. Other providers are planned.

Does Titan Zero send texts for me?

No. Texts are sent through your own Twilio account, and you pay Twilio directly.

Terms and privacy

Where are the terms for the Scheduling & Enquiries plugin?

In its terms and conditions. The general Titan Zero terms and Fair Use Policy also apply.

Where is the privacy policy?

On the privacy page.
