Scheduling & Enquiries: frequently asked questions

Getting started

What is Scheduling & Enquiries?

A free WordPress plugin for service businesses such as plumbers, cleaners, salons, tutors and clinics. It takes bookings, keeps calendars, customers and reminders, and handles enquiries with built-in rules.

Is it free?

Yes. Bookings, calendars, customers, reminders, reports and rules-based enquiry handling are free. The plugin needs no licence key and no account.

What does my site need to run it?

WordPress 6.6 or later and PHP 7.4 or later. It has been tested on WordPress 6.6.2 and 7.1.4.

Does it need other Titan Zero plugins?

No. It runs standalone, without any other Titan Zero plugin.

Is it on WordPress.org yet?

Not yet. Ask our support team for the current download.

How do I set it up?

Install and activate the plugin, then use the setup wizard to enter your business details, services, working hours and calendars. Publish your booking form on a page, and create the booking page and client portal from Settings.

Can I import bookings from Appointment Hour Booking or Titan Front Desk?

Not automatically. There is no import tool in this version. Keep the old plugin until you have what you need, then enter any remaining bookings by hand.

Bookings and calendars

What can customers do on the booking form?

Customers choose a free time slot for a service. Booking rules check working hours, durations and capacity before the booking is saved.

Can I have more than one calendar or staff member?

Yes. Set working hours, service durations and staff or resource capacity for each calendar.

How do customers change or cancel a booking?

They sign in to the client portal with their email address. Customers can view, reschedule or cancel their upcoming bookings there.

How do I see my bookings?

Use the admin calendar or the appointment list. You can change a booking's status there, and export appointments to CSV.

Do reminders and follow-ups go out automatically?

Yes. Email notifications, customer reminders and follow-ups are sent for each booking. Reminders are re-queued after a customer reschedules or a status changes.

Does it include quotes or invoices?

No. Quotes and invoices are not part of the free plugin, and they are not needed for booking. Quotes & Pricing and Invoices & Payments are separate plugins.

Enquiries and industry packs

How are enquiries handled without AI?

Rules-based enquiry handling answers questions and routes enquiries using the rules you set. It is part of the free plugin.

What are industry packs?

Packs of example services and answers for a trade or service type. Each pack is a starting point. Review and edit every answer before you publish your form.

Can I write my own pack?

Yes. Custom packs can be uploaded or registered.

AI upgrades

Is the free plugin AI-powered?

It includes rules-based enquiry handling and a limited free AI allowance. Hosted AI only runs after you connect it and allow it in the plugin.

What is the AI Assistant?

A hosted conversational AI service. It answers enquiries on your site, guides customers to a booking, drafts replies and quotes, and writes owner summaries. Price: A$29 per month, including GST.

What is the AI Agent Team?

A hosted team of specialist agents for enquiries, booking and calendar work, quote intake and approved follow-up. It includes the AI Assistant. Price: A$99 per month, including GST.

Do I need both?

No. The AI Agent Team includes the AI Assistant.

Will the agents act without asking me?

No. They work within the approvals you set, ask you before any decision that needs one, and record what they did.

Are the AI upgrades available now?

No. They are in development and not yet available to buy.

What happens if I cancel?

The site returns to the free plugin and its free AI allowance. Your bookings and settings are kept.

Text messages

Which SMS provider can I use?

Twilio, using your own account. Twilio is the only SMS provider supported today. Other providers are planned.

Who pays for texts?

You do. Texts are sent through your own Twilio account, and Twilio bills you directly.

Is SMS on by default?

No. SMS stays off until your site administrator adds your Twilio details.

What texts does the plugin send?

Text-back to missed calls, and approved callback or escalation messages. Live voice and SMS for the Agent Team will use the same Twilio setup when they are available.

Pricing and billing

Is there a Pro plugin?

No. There is no Pro plugin or Pro licence. The free plugin is the whole plugin, and the AI upgrades are added inside it.

How are the AI upgrades billed?

As monthly subscriptions, in Australian dollars including GST. You cancel in your Titan Zero account, and cancellation takes effect from the next billing date.

Do Twilio charges come from Titan Zero?

No. Twilio bills you directly for your own account.

Data and privacy

Where is my data stored?

In your WordPress database. The free plugin needs no account and sends nothing to Titan Zero.

Does Titan Zero see my bookings or customers?

Not unless you connect a hosted AI service and allow it. Only then is the information needed for a request sent to that service.

Does the plugin store IP addresses?

Yes, the IP address of each booking request is stored by default. You can turn this off under Booking rules.

What happens to my data if I uninstall?

Your bookings and settings are kept unless you enable the data-deletion setting described in the plugin's own documentation.

How do I erase a customer's data?

Use WordPress's personal data erase tool. It deletes the booking records that match the customer's email address, and removes their client-portal sign-in links and sessions.

Support and licence

How do I get help?

Email support@titanzero.io. Include your WordPress and PHP versions, and what you expected to happen.

How do I report a security problem?

Email support@titanzero.io. Please do not post exploit details publicly.

What licence is it under?

GPL-2.0-only. The source is public, and you may use and modify it under that licence.

Where are the terms?

In the plugin terms and conditions. The Titan Zero terms and Fair Use Policy also apply.
