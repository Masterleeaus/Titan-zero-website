FRONT DESK

Free booking and enquiries for your WordPress site. No AI needed.

Front Desk is a free WordPress plugin for service businesses. It takes bookings, keeps your calendar and customer list, and answers enquiries with simple rules. AI is optional. Add a hosted AI service later if you want more help.

Install the free pluginCompare the AI options

Front Desk is the Scheduling & Enquiries Manager plugin. It is not yet on WordPress.org, so support sends you the current download.

THE FREE PLUGIN

The free plugin runs your bookings.

Bookings, calendars, customers, reminders and reports work on your own WordPress site. None of them are locked behind a paid version or a licence key.

Booking form and calendars

Customers pick a free time on your form. You set working hours, how long each service takes, and how many bookings each staff member can take.

Booking page and client portal

Settings creates the booking page in your theme. Clients sign in with their email address to view, change or cancel bookings.

Customers and services

Customer records with addresses and notes, and a list of the services you offer.

Enquiries and reminders

Simple rules handle enquiries. Email notifications, customer reminders and follow-ups go out for each booking.

Admin calendar and reports

See every appointment, change its status, and export the list to CSV. A dashboard widget shows the day at a glance.

A small free AI allowance

Basic AI help for enquiries is included. It only runs after you connect hosted AI and allow it in the plugin.

TEXT MESSAGES

Texts go through your own Twilio account.

Text-back to missed calls uses your own Twilio account. You pay Twilio directly, and your messages do not pass through a Titan account.

  • Twilio is the only SMS provider supported today. Other providers are planned.
  • SMS stays off until your site administrator adds your Twilio details.
  • Voice and SMS for the Agent Team will use the same Twilio setup when they are live.

AI IS OPTIONAL

Two optional AI services. Pick one.

Bookings work without AI. If you want more help with enquiries, add one of two hosted services. Each runs alongside the same plugin. Neither is a second plugin, and both are in development, so you cannot buy them yet.

WHO IT IS FOR

Service businesses that take bookings.

Front Desk suits cleaning, trades, handyman, gardening, beauty and mobile service businesses. The free plugin handles the bookings for each of them. AI services are optional and add help with enquiries.

  • Cleaning: the booking form, calendars and customer records work on the free plugin. The Assistant can answer questions about your services.
  • Trades: working hours and staff capacity for call-outs work on the free plugin. The Agent Team can handle enquiries and calendar changes, and you approve each change that needs it.
  • Handyman and property maintenance: bookings, reminders and customer notes work on the free plugin. The Assistant can guide a customer to a booking time that fits your rules.
  • Gardening and landscaping: your service list and calendar rules for each crew work on the free plugin. The Assistant can answer enquiries from the service information you enter.
  • Beauty and personal services: appointments, reminders and a client portal work on the free plugin. Clients change or cancel their own bookings.
  • Mobile services: customer records, with addresses and notes, work on the free plugin. Agent Team bookings follow the calendar and capacity rules you set.

Industry packs are starting points. Check the example services and answers, and change them, before you publish your booking form.

PRIVACY AND DATA

Your data stays in your WordPress database.

The free plugin has no account and no licence key. Bookings and customer details are stored in your own database.

  • Hosted AI stays off until you connect it and allow it. Only then is a request sent to the hosted service.
  • Text messages use your own Twilio account, which you control and pay for.
  • Each booking request stores the customer's IP address by default. You can turn this off under Booking rules.
  • Uninstalling keeps your bookings and settings, unless you turn on the data-deletion setting described in the plugin's FAQ.

Full details are in the Terms and the Fair Use Policy.

FAQ

Common questions.

Does the free plugin need AI?

No. It uses simple rules for enquiries and includes a small free AI allowance. Hosted AI only runs after you connect it and allow it in the plugin.

Do I need to pay to run bookings?

No. Bookings, calendars, customers and reminders are free. They do not depend on either AI service.

Which SMS provider can I use?

Twilio, using your own account. Other SMS providers are planned but not yet supported.

Do I need both AI services?

No. The Agent Team includes the Assistant. Choose the Agent Team only if you want the extra agents.

Can I move data from Appointment Hour Booking or Titan Front Desk?

Not automatically. This version has no import tool. Keep the old plugin until you have what you need, then enter any remaining bookings by hand.

Where do I get help?

Email support@titanzero.io. For security problems, email the same address and do not post exploit details publicly.
