FRONT DESK

Scheduling and enquiries for service businesses, free to start.

Front Desk is the Scheduling & Enquiries Manager WordPress plugin. It takes bookings, keeps your calendars and customers, and answers enquiries with built-in rules and a limited free AI allowance. You can add a hosted AI service when you need more.

Ask for the free pluginExplore upgrades

The free plugin is not yet listed on WordPress.org. Ask our support team for the current download.

THE FREE PLUGIN

Everything local is included, with no licence key.

Booking, calendars, customers, reminders and reports work on your own WordPress site. None of these features are locked behind a paid version.

Booking form and calendars

Customers choose a free time from your form. Set working hours, durations and staff or resource capacity.

Booking page and client portal

Created from Settings, in your theme's layout. Clients sign in with their email address to view, change or cancel bookings.

Customers and services

Local customer records, addresses, notes and a service catalogue for your calendars.

Enquiries and reminders

Rules-based enquiry handling, email notifications, customer reminders and follow-ups.

Admin calendar and reports

Appointment list, status changes, a dashboard widget and CSV export.

A limited free AI allowance

Basic AI help for enquiries is included. It uses hosted AI only after you connect it and allow it in the plugin.

TEXT MESSAGES

Bring your own Twilio account.

Text-back to missed calls and approved callback or escalation messages send through your own Twilio account. You pay Twilio directly, and your messages never pass through a Titan account.

  • Twilio is the only SMS provider supported today. Other providers are planned.
  • SMS is off until your site administrator adds your Twilio details.
  • Live voice and SMS for the Agent Team will use the same Twilio setup when they are available.

WHO IT IS FOR

Service businesses that take bookings.

Front Desk suits cleaning, trades, handyman, gardening, beauty and mobile service businesses. The free plugin does the booking work for each of them. The optional AI services add help with enquiries and quotes, and each one needs your approval where a decision needs one.

  • Cleaning: The booking form, calendars and customer records work on the free plugin. When the Assistant is available, it can answer questions about your services and draft quotes for you to review.
  • Trades: Set working hours and staff capacity for callouts on the free plugin. When the Agent Team is available, it can collect job details for quote intake, and you approve every quote before it goes out.
  • Handyman and property maintenance: Bookings, reminders and customer notes work on the free plugin. When the Assistant is available, it can guide a customer to a booking time within your rules.
  • Gardening and landscaping: A service catalogue and calendar rules for each staff member or crew work on the free plugin. When the Assistant is available, it can answer enquiries using the service information you enter.
  • Beauty and personal services: Appointments, reminders and a client portal where customers view, change or cancel bookings work on the free plugin. When the Agent Team is available, it can send follow-ups only to clients who have agreed to them.
  • Mobile services: Local customer records, including addresses and notes, work on the free plugin. When the Agent Team is available, its bookings follow the same calendar and capacity rules you set.

Approval boundaries

  • Local booking features keep working whether or not a hosted AI service is connected.
  • Hosted AI runs only after you connect it and allow it in the plugin.
  • Planned: quotes drafted by the Agent Team go out only after you approve them.
  • Planned: the Assistant and Agent Team propose booking times within your rules. You approve a booking before it is confirmed.
  • Planned: follow-up messages go only to customers who have agreed to receive them.

Each industry pack is a starting point. Check the example services and answers, and change them before you publish your booking form. The AI services are in development and not yet available to buy.

FROM ENQUIRY TO JOB

One customer path, from first enquiry to finished job.

Front Desk handles the enquiry and the booking inside your own WordPress site. Each stage shows what the free plugin does on its own and what the optional hosted services add.

  1. Enquiry: the customer sends a request through your form. The free plugin applies your enquiry rules, sends notifications and keeps the customer record. When the Assistant is available, it can answer questions from your service information. When the Agent Team is available, it can collect job details and prepare a reply for your approval.
  2. Booking: the customer picks a free time from your calendar, within your working hours and staff or resource capacity. The free plugin confirms the booking and sends reminders. The client portal lets customers view, change or cancel their bookings. When the Assistant is available, it can guide a customer to a time within your rules.
  3. Job delivery: the booking appears in your admin calendar and reports. Dispatch, job sheets and invoicing are not part of the free plugin. The connected app SortedAt.Work covers jobs and field operations, but that connection is not live yet.

Connected-app links are planned future integrations, not current features. Hosted AI does not run until you connect it and allow it in the plugin.

PRIVACY AND DATA

Your data stays in your WordPress database.

Front Desk does not create an account or collect a licence key for the free plugin. Bookings and customer details are stored in your database.

  • Hosted AI is off until you connect it and allow it. Only then is the request sent to the hosted service.
  • Text messages use your own Twilio account, which you control and pay for.
  • The IP address of each booking request is stored by default. You can turn this off under Booking rules.
  • Uninstalling keeps your bookings and settings unless you add the data-deletion constant described in the plugin's FAQ.

Full details are in the Terms and the Fair Use Policy.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is the free plugin really AI-powered?

It includes built-in rules-based enquiry handling and a limited free AI allowance. Hosted AI only runs after you connect it and allow it in the plugin.

Do I need to pay to run bookings?

No. Bookings, calendars, customers and reminders are free and do not depend on either AI service.

Which SMS provider can I use?

Twilio, using your own account. Other SMS providers are planned but not yet supported.

Do I need both AI services?

No. The Agent Team includes the Assistant, so you only need the Agent Team if you want the extra agents.

Can I move data from Appointment Hour Booking or Titan Front Desk?

Not automatically. There is no import tool in this version, so keep the old plugin until you have what you need, then enter any remaining bookings by hand.

Where do I get help?

Email support@titanzero.io. For security problems, email the same address and do not post exploit details publicly.

Who owns the booking and customer data?

You do. Booking, customer and reporting data is stored in your WordPress database. Titan Zero does not receive that data unless you connect a hosted AI service and allow it.

Can I export my data?

This version has no export tool described on this page. Because the data is in your own WordPress database, your usual WordPress backup and database tools can read it. Ask support@titanzero.io before you rely on a specific export.

What happens to hosted AI if I stop paying for it?

Stopping a hosted AI subscription returns the site to the free AI allowance. Bookings, calendars and customer records do not depend on the Assistant or the Agent Team.

How do I stop using Front Desk?

This page does not describe how stored data is handled when the plugin is removed. Ask support@titanzero.io before you remove the plugin from a live site, so you can keep what you need.

What does each AI service cost?

The Assistant is A$29 per month, and the Agent Team is A$99 per month, both including GST. The Agent Team includes the Assistant. Both are in development and not yet available to buy.
