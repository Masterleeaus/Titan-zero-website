Titan BOS/AI workforce

THE WORKFORCE WITHIN TITAN BOS

Not an AI bolted on.
A workforce with a job.

Titan BOS is the operating system. Its AI workforce helps with the operational work inside it: coordinating priorities, handling defined workflows and carrying out authorised tasks.

01

Manager

Coordinates a business function and keeps priorities and exceptions visible.

02

Specialist

Owns a defined workflow, such as booking, scheduling or billing.

03

Worker

Carries out a bounded task inside the permissions and rules you set.

CHOOSE HOW MUCH RESPONSIBILITY TO HAND OVER

Assistance first.
Authority by choice.

Compare operating levels
01

Assist

You work. Titan helps.

On-demand drafting, summaries, analysis and next-step preparation.

02

Suggest

Titan watches. You decide.

Proactive monitoring, useful signals and recommended actions for your review.

03

Autonomous

Titan works within your rules.

Authorised routine actions run within the boundaries you configure.

04

Predictive + Autonomous

Titan looks ahead.

Predictive signals can trigger responses that are already authorised.

Prediction does not grant permission. A risk signal can suggest a next step without giving the system permission to change a price, send a message or issue a refund.
