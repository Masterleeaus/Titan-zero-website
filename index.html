BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

Common questions,
answered plainly.

Here is what to know about the apps, costs and next steps.

Ask us a question
Are all six At.Work apps the same product?

No. Each app is a separate experience in the Titan Zero family. Each one is built for a different part of running a service business.

Do I need every app?

Start with the workflow that matters most. Your setup review will show which apps fit your business.

Is Titan Zero for solo operators?

Yes. SoloAt.Work is built for people who run their own work. The other apps are built for teams and office work.

Does everything connect automatically?

Connections depend on the services you use and how the setup is configured. Your setup should list what is connected and what still needs setting up.

Is AI included in my plan?

AI is included in the Standard AI and Unlimited AI tiers for each app. The bring-your-own tier uses your own AI keys.

Is the setup review a trial of paid software?

No. The website and setup review is a proposal. You can read it before you decide.

Can I see exactly what will be included?

Yes. Your proposal lists the website services, core tools, optional extras and ongoing charges before you accept.

How do I get started?

Tell the Titan Zero team what your business does and which job needs fixing. We can discuss the apps, setup and next steps. This website does not create an account for you automatically.
