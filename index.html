HOW IT WORKS

A practical starting point, built around your business.

01 — Tell us how you work. Share your services, how you handle enquiries, whether you work alone or with a team, and what needs attention first.

02 — Review the proposed setup. See a website and system arrangement tailored to your business, with a clear explanation of what is included and what is optional.

03 — Choose when to go ahead. If the setup suits your needs, activate the agreed services and move ahead with support for implementation.

See the business. Make the call.

Bring priorities, customer activity and work into the owner’s view. Review what needs attention and make the next decision without piecing the story together from separate tools.

  • Daily priorities across the business
  • Decisions and approvals that need you
  • Customers and work in context

Connect the plan to the work.

Use the SortedAt.Work workspace to coordinate schedules, assignments and job progress. Keep the office connected to the team on site, with the customer details, work requirements and completion records in the same operational picture.

Make the next step clear for customers.

Give customers the information relevant to their service, rather than your internal tools. Keep requests, booking details and customer-facing follow-up connected to the work your business is delivering.

  • Service requests and customer interactions
  • Relevant booking and work information
  • Customer-facing updates and follow-up

Give the person on site the right context.

Bring the assignment, job details and progress recording to the person doing the work. Keep checklists, notes and evidence with the job, so the office can see the outcome without rebuilding the story.

  • Assigned jobs and relevant site details
  • Checklists, notes and evidence
  • Progress and completion records

Ask us your practical questions
Are all six At.Work apps the same product?

No. They are six focused experiences in the Titan Zero brand family, each designed around a different part of running a service business.

Do I need every app?

Start with the workflows that matter to your business. The right combination depends on your needs and which services are available to you.

Is Titan Zero for solo operators?

Yes. SoloAt.Work is the focused experience designed for people running their own work. Other At.Work products address team and office workflows.

Does everything sync automatically?

Integration depends on the connected services and the deployed configuration. The setup process should clearly identify what is connected and what needs configuration.

Is AI included in my plan?

AI is included in the Standard AI and Unlimited AI tiers for each app. The bring-your-own tier uses your own AI keys.

Is the setup review a trial of paid software?

No. The website and setup review is a proposal you can examine before deciding whether to go ahead.

Can I see exactly what will be included?

Yes. The proposal should identify website services, included core tools, optional products and ongoing charges before acceptance.

How do I get started?

Tell the Titan Zero team what your business does and which workflow needs attention. We can discuss the relevant apps, configuration, availability and next steps. This website does not create an account automatically.
