TITAN BOS — YOUR BUSINESS WORKFLOW CHECKLIST
By Titan Zero | Business Operating System

Use this sheet to prepare a useful setup conversation.
You do not need all five apps to begin.

1. START WITH ONE REAL CUSTOMER JOURNEY
What service is the customer asking for?
Where does their enquiry arrive?
Who answers it, and who owns the next step?
What information is copied or asked for twice?

2. BUILD — DEFINE THE OPPORTUNITY
List the service, service area, operating hours and scope.
Note pricing inputs and booking constraints.
Describe the customers and markets you want to reach.
Identify what must be handed to the front office.

3. SUPERCHARGED AT WORK — KEEP THE CUSTOMER CONTEXT
Name the person responsible for enquiries and follow-up.
List the details needed before quoting or booking.
Identify where accepted scope and customer instructions are recorded.
Define when a request is ready for SortedAt.Work.

4. SORTED AT WORK — MAKE DELIVERY CLEAR
List the planning, assignment and on-site steps.
Identify the checklist, evidence and completion information needed.
Decide who handles changes, exceptions and quality review.

5. SET THE BOUNDARIES
Which tasks should be draft-only?
Which need human approval before execution?
What is allowed to run automatically, and within which limits?
Who handles exceptions? What records must be kept?

7. CHOOSE YOUR STARTING POINT
Start with the handoff that creates the most repeated work.
Choose the relevant app and operating level; confirm availability.
App subscriptions, provider usage, taxes and setup services are separate considerations.
