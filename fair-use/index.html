Fair Use Policy

Unlimited AI is designed for real business use. This policy explains the monthly fair use cap and how we keep the Services reliable for everyone. Last updated 11 November 2026.

1. Scope

This policy applies to every Titan BOS app with an AI plan: SuperchargedAt.Work, SortedAt.Work, ScheduledAt.Work, SyncedAt.Work and SoloAt.Work. It is part of the Terms and conditions.

2. Bring your own AI and API keys

You supply your own AI model or API keys, and your provider bills your AI usage directly. Smart Task limits do not apply to that usage, but the acceptable use rules in section 7 still apply to how you use the Services.

3. Standard AI

Standard AI includes up to 200 Smart Tasks per day for each app. A Smart Task is one AI-completed action, such as drafting a reply, building a quote or booking a job. Standard AI does not carry over unused Smart Tasks.

4. Unlimited AI and the monthly fair use cap

Unlimited AI has no fixed daily limit. It is subject to a fair use cap of 150,000 Smart Tasks per month for each business account, counted across all apps on that account. The cap resets once each month.

5. Normal business use

Normal business use means your team running its own operations: answering enquiries, managing jobs, sending quotes and invoices, and running campaigns that meet the Spam Act 2003 (Cth) consent and unsubscribe requirements.

6. What we do when the cap is reached

  • Approaching the cap: we will tell the account owner by email when usage reaches 80% of the monthly cap.
  • At the cap: AI actions on that account pause until the cap resets or the plan changes. Core app features that do not use AI keep working.
  • Sustained use above the cap: we may contact you to discuss a higher plan, a dedicated arrangement or a change to how the automation runs.

7. Activity that is not allowed

  • Automated scripts or bots that generate Smart Tasks for purposes other than your own business.
  • Reselling, sharing or sublicensing AI access to other businesses or individuals.
  • Using the Services to scrape or harvest data, or to run spam or unsolicited campaigns.
  • Deliberately splitting one business across several accounts to avoid the cap.
  • Attempts to overload, bypass or interfere with the Services or their security.

8. Measuring usage

Usage is measured by Smart Tasks recorded in your account. If you think your usage has been recorded incorrectly, contact us and we will review the records for your account.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Where a change affects your plan, we will give you at least 30 days' notice.

10. Questions

Contact support@titanzero.io. See the Terms and conditions.
