FREE INSTALLATION

Install the free plugin.

The free plugin is not yet listed on WordPress.org. Ask support for the current download, then install it on your WordPress site like any other plugin file.

  1. Activate the plugin. No licence key is needed.
  2. Set your working hours, services, and staff or resource capacity.
  3. Create the booking page from Settings, then check the booking form with a test booking before you publish it.

Bookings, calendars, customers, reminders and reports work on the free plugin and do not depend on any hosted service.

VERSION AND UPGRADE COMPATIBILITY

Versions and upgrades.

No version matrix is published yet. WordPress version requirements and the upgrade path will be listed here once they are verified against the released plugin.

  • Back up your site and bookings before you update the plugin.
  • There is no Pro plugin or Pro download. The older Pro page is kept only for historical reference.

HOSTED AI ASSISTANT OPT-IN

Turn on the Assistant only if you want it.

Titan Zero AI Assistant is an optional hosted service at A$29 per month, incl. GST. It is in development and not yet available to buy.

  • Hosted AI stays off until you connect it and allow it in the plugin.
  • A request is sent to the hosted service only after you allow it.
  • The free plugin keeps its limited AI allowance without the Assistant.

Details of the service are on the AI Assistant page.

AGENT TEAM PERMISSIONS

What the Agent Team may do.

Titan Zero AI Agent Team is an optional hosted service at A$99 per month, incl. GST. It includes the Assistant. It is in development and not yet available to buy, so the rules below describe the intended permissions.

  • Agents work within the calendar and capacity rules you set.
  • You approve every quote before it goes out.
  • Follow-ups go only to clients who have agreed to them.

Details of the service are on the AI Agent Team page.

PRIVACY AND CONSENT

Your data and your choices.

  • Bookings and customer details are stored in your WordPress database.
  • The IP address of each booking request is stored by default. You can turn this off under Booking rules.
  • Text messages use your own Twilio account, which you control and pay for.
  • Uninstalling keeps your bookings and settings unless you add the data-deletion constant described in the plugin FAQ.

Full terms are in the Terms, the Fair Use Policy and the website privacy notice.

BILLING AND CANCELLATION

No billing is live yet.

Nothing is charged for the free plugin, and no hosted subscription is currently open for purchase, so there is nothing to cancel. When billing opens, this page will explain how to cancel and when access ends. Ask support about any service before then.

TROUBLESHOOTING

Common checks.

  • Booking times missing: check working hours and staff or resource capacity.
  • Text messages not sending: SMS stays off until your Twilio details are added.
  • Assistant not responding: hosted AI does nothing until it is connected and allowed.

For anything not listed, contact support and include your WordPress and plugin versions.

SERVICE STATUS

Service status.

No live status feed is published yet, so this page does not report uptime or incidents. A status record will be added once the hosted services are monitored. To report a problem now, use support.
