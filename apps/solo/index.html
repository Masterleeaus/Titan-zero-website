SOLO AT WORK · PART OF TITAN BOS

One focused place for the business you're building yourself.

SoloAt.Work adds departments of AI agents to your business. They answer enquiries, follow up quotes, schedule jobs, chase invoices and forecast what needs attention, so you can stay on the work.

Explore SoloAt.Work See pricing

Predictive automation with your approval on the decisions that matter. Connected to the Business Operating System.

THE JOB OF SOLOAT.WORK

The back office, run by agents you can see and control.

A solo operator is the front desk, the salesperson, the bookkeeper and the marketer at once. SoloAt.Work splits that work across departments of agents, so the admin still gets done.

Predictive automation looks ahead at the coming week's jobs, cash and follow-ups, and acts on them before they become problems.

WHAT SOLOAT.WORK HELPS YOU DO

Departments that work the business while you work.

Front desk agents.

Answer enquiries and calls, book jobs into your calendar, and pass anything that needs your judgement to you.

Sales and finance agents.

Turn enquiries into quotes, follow them up until the customer decides, then invoice and chase payment.

Predictive operations.

Forecast next week's workload and cash flow, flag jobs running over budget, and schedule the work before the gap appears.
