THE JOB OF SUPERCHARGED AT WORK

Know the customer before you move the work.

A message is not just a message. It might be a new job, a change to a booking or a customer who needs help. SuperchargedAt.Work keeps the request, the relationship and the next action together.

Use SuperchargedAt.Work where you need it now. Connect additional Titan BOS workflows when the business is ready.

WHAT SUPERCHARGED AT WORK HELPS YOU DO

Every enquiry deserves a clear next step.

Answer enquiries around the clock.

Incoming agents handle calls and chats 24/7, route each enquiry, capture the details you need and offer self-service for common questions.

Keep one record for every customer.

A CRM with contacts, history and segments, plus duplicate detection, so every conversation adds to the same customer record.

Follow quotes until they are decided.

Quote follow-up, invoices and payments, with a review requested after each job.

Book, reschedule and remind.

Bookings with calendar sync, confirmations and reminders, plus emergency and after-hours triage.

Run campaigns with care.

Email, SMS and WhatsApp campaigns with consent checks, quiet hours and opt-out keywords, all sent within calling windows you set.

Hand the work over with a clear brief.

AI roles hand work to each other with defined limits, escalate with a recovery path, and send consequential actions to a person for approval.

THE FULL SUPERCHARGED AT WORK FEATURE SET

Everything the front office needs, in one workforce.

AI calling

  • Natural agents with a custom personality, voice and knowledge
  • Flow agents with drag-and-drop call scripts
  • 24/7 incoming agents with routing and self-service
  • Outbound campaigns with timezone-aware calling windows, concurrency limits, pause and resume, and automatic retries
  • Phone numbers in 100+ countries, with caller ID and inbound routing
  • Live call monitoring and a visual log of each call

Chat and inbox

  • Chatbots with an AI builder, conversation history and training
  • Web chat, WhatsApp, Telegram, Messenger and Instagram
  • A realtime inbox with live updates
  • Voice mode with daily limits
  • Content generation and an order flow with payment status

Knowledge

  • Upload PDF, Word, text and web pages
  • Content is searched during calls and chats

Bookings, quotes and invoices

  • Booking and rescheduling with confirmations and reminders
  • Quotes, quote follow-up, invoices and reviews after each job
  • Materials and parts tracking, and service agreements with renewal reminders
  • Compliance tracking for certificates and licences

Payments and billing

  • Stripe, Razorpay, PayPal, Paystack, MercadoPago and YooKassa
  • Credits at one per call minute, with low-balance warnings and auto-pause
  • Subscription plans, one-time purchases, invoices and PDF receipts
  • Refunds and multi-currency

Customers and compliance

  • A CRM with contacts, history and segments
  • KYC review with required documents and an approval workflow
  • Complaints, refunds, disputes and warranty callbacks
  • Lapsed-customer reactivation and referral requests

AI workforce

  • 125 roles, plus executive and system roles, in 17 families and 15 divisions
  • 62 teams, with templates for four business sizes
  • Four autonomy levels: assist, suggest, auto and predictive
  • Goals tracked and verified, with budgets and cost receipts per role
  • Handoffs, delegation limits and escalation with recovery
  • Learning from outcomes, with improvement proposals
  • Each client receives only the roles it needs

Approvals and control

  • An approval queue where a person approves or rejects each consequential action
  • Policies that set who may act on money, customer messages and deletions
  • Signed approval decisions, with expiry and resubmission rules
  • Output redaction for secrets and personal data, and a credential vault
  • An audit log with tamper-evident hashing and export
  • Soft delete and recovery for data privacy

Platform

  • Login with roles, and per-company data separation
  • Email and notifications with routing and escalation
  • Interface in 11 languages, with right-to-left layouts
  • Outgoing webhooks with signed payloads and delivery logs
  • Offline mode for mobile, with sync when back online

Features vary by plan, configuration and release. Some capabilities are being rolled out in stages.
