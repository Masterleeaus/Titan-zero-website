THE JOB OF SCHEDULED AT WORK

Get the offer ready before customers arrive.

A new service needs clear details, times customers can book, and a page that explains it. ScheduledAt.Work handles all three in one place.

Start with ScheduledAt.Work on its own. Add other Titan BOS apps when the business is ready for them.

WHAT BUILD HELPS YOU DO

Set up a new service from one place.

Make the business model clear.

Organise business details, service areas, operating hours and service options, so customers know what you offer and when they can book.

Set up services customers can book.

Define each service, its scope and the booking rules that apply, before a new offer reaches the customer.

Build the pages that explain each service.

Create your website and landing pages around the services and areas you serve, with the details a customer needs to book.

Set up the markets you serve.

Define the areas, markets and service mix your business works in, so each service has a clear place to sell.

WITH FRONT DESK

How ScheduledAt.Work connects to Front Desk.

  • Front Desk Free: Takes bookings and enquiries from your WordPress site. Calendars, customer details and reminders are stored in your own database.
  • ScheduledAt.Work adds: Advanced scheduling and capacity planning across more staff and jobs.
  • Typical workflow: A booking request arrives in Front Desk and is checked against the calendar. Once you connect the two, ScheduledAt.Work adds capacity planning.
  • Consent: nothing moves between Front Desk and ScheduledAt.Work until you connect them and agree to what each side shares. No connection is live yet.
