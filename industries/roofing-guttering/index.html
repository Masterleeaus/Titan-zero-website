Roofing & Guttering business tools and planning

Estimate roofing and guttering project capacity using crew hours, materials, access, weather delays and project duration.

What to include in the plan

Roofing and guttering work is often project-based. Estimate crew hours, material quantities, site access, weather exposure and the time between stages rather than treating a project like a short call-out.

  • Use a project-specific scope and include access, setup, clean-up and disposal in the duration.
  • Separate materials and direct crew costs from fixed vehicle, insurance and business overhead.
  • Leave realistic schedule contingency for weather and site conditions; the simple capacity estimate cannot predict them.

The calculator values below are illustrative starter assumptions only. They are not market averages, price recommendations or compliance advice. Replace them with your own records before relying on a result.
