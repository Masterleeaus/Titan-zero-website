Industry guide

Appliance and equipment repair: plan repair calls

Plan repair calls around diagnosis time, parts, travel, return visits and technician time.

What to plan for

Repairs can include diagnosis, parts, customer approval, a return visit and warranty questions. Record each stage that changes time or cost.

  • Track diagnostic visits apart from finished repairs, so average time reflects the real mix.
  • Track parts cost, travel and return visits against finished job records.
  • Keep warranty and customer-supplied-parts scenarios in view when you prepare a quote.

The numbers below are starter examples only. They are not market averages, price advice or compliance advice. Replace them with your own records before you rely on a result.
