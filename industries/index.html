Industries

Different services. One connected operation.

Explore the job patterns that change pricing, team capacity and customer follow-up. Each page includes practical prompts and calculator defaults that you can replace with your own figures.

01

Cleaning

Cleaning businesses often balance repeat bookings with travel time, access details, consumables and quality checks. A small change to route density or job duration can change the hours available for new work.

Explore this service type →

02

Plumbing

Plumbing work can mix scheduled maintenance with variable call-outs. Diagnosis, travel, parts availability and approved scope all affect the time and contribution a job leaves.

Explore this service type →

03

Electrical

Electrical service businesses need a job model that includes travel, material handling, scope changes and time for safe completion records. Compare the estimate with the actual work delivered.

Explore this service type →

04

HVAC

HVAC demand and job duration can vary across the year and between servicing, repair and installation. Model each work type with its own duration and direct costs where possible.

Explore this service type →

05

Handyman & Property Maintenance

Property maintenance often combines small repairs and multi-task visits. The main planning challenge is the variation in task duration, access, materials and the order in which jobs can be grouped.

Explore this service type →

06

Landscaping & Gardening

Landscaping and garden maintenance may involve crews, equipment, green-waste disposal and weather-driven changes. Track the crew hours and materials used by each kind of visit.

Explore this service type →

07

Pest Control

Pest-control operations often combine one-off treatments with scheduled follow-up or recurring programs. The type of treatment, travel and preparation can change both job duration and materials.

Explore this service type →

08

Locksmith & Security

Locksmith and security work can include scheduled installation, urgent call-outs and hardware changes. Travel, parts selection, response expectations and after-hours availability affect the job economics.

Explore this service type →

09

Roofing & Guttering

Roofing and guttering work is often project-based. Estimate crew hours, material quantities, site access, weather exposure and the time between stages rather than treating a project like a short call-out.

Explore this service type →

10

Appliance & Equipment Repair

Repair work can include diagnosis, parts sourcing, customer approval, a return visit and warranty questions. Record these stages separately where they materially affect time or cost.

Explore this service type →
