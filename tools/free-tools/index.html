Independent resources

Free tools we recommend.

Start with official guidance and useful no-cost resources before paying for another system. We label free templates, calculators, government services and provider plans plainly.

These links are independent services. Titan Zero is not affiliated with or paid by the providers listed here. Free access, eligibility, features and terms can change; check each provider before relying on a tool.

Business planning

Financial tools and templates ↗

business.gov.au · Australian Government

Free templates and practical guides

Find Australian small-business budget, cash-flow, profit-and-loss and balance-sheet guidance and templates.

Check: Templates help organise information; they are not accounting or tax advice.

Business plan tool ↗

business.gov.au · Australian Government

Free interactive tool

Work through a business plan for a new idea or an existing business and download the resulting plan.

Check: The government notes that the tool requires cookies and JavaScript; use the downloadable template if it does not suit your browser.

Start-up costs sheet ↗

business.gov.au · Australian Government

Free spreadsheet template

List the costs that arise before a business begins earning income, then use the template to plan the initial funding requirement.

Check: The right cost categories and tax treatment depend on your business and circumstances.

Tax and records

ATO calculators and tools ↗

Australian Taxation Office

Free official tools

Find official calculators and tools for business tax, super and record-keeping questions.

Check: Use current ATO guidance for your obligations; an estimate is not a ruling or tailored tax advice.

GST calculator ↗

ASIC MoneySmart

Free calculator

Check the GST component of an amount using a simple Australian GST calculator.

Check: Use ATO guidance to determine whether GST applies to your supply and what you must report.

People and pay

Pay and Conditions Tool ↗

Fair Work Ombudsman · Australian Government

Free government calculator

Check pay rates, allowances, penalty rates, leave, notice and redundancy scenarios against the Fair Work information available in the tool.

Check: Correct award coverage, classification and current employment details matter. Confirm the applicable rules before making payroll decisions.

Licences and permits

Australian Business Licence and Information Service ↗

ABLIS · Australian Government

Free government search service

Search for licences, permits and regulatory information that may apply to a business and its location in Australia.

Check: Use the relevant regulator to confirm requirements for your activity, state or territory and local area.

Local visibility

Google Business Profile ↗

Google

No-charge profile; eligibility and verification apply

Manage how an eligible local or service-area business appears on Google Search and Maps, including hours, contact details, photos and reviews.

Check: A profile is not a ranking guarantee. Google sets eligibility, verification and content rules.
