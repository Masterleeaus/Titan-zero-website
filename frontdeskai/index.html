HOW IT DIFFERS

Front Desk AI assists you. Front Desk Agent acts for you.

Front Desk AI works mainly for the business owner and staff. It understands information, makes recommendations and prepares actions for approval. Front Desk Agent carries out delegated tasks on its own, within defined permissions.

EXAMPLE

A booking enquiry, handled with approval.

Illustrative example. This is a worked example, not a live transcript.

Customer: “I need a cleaner for my three-bedroom house next Thursday morning. How much would it cost?”

  • Identifies a residential cleaning service
  • Asks for the property suburb and cleaning requirements
  • Checks matching availability
  • Prepares a draft estimate using approved rates
  • Presents the recommendation for confirmation

No booking is created automatically.

HUMAN APPROVAL AND PRIVACY

You stay in control of every action.

AI suggestions are reviewed by you before anything is booked, quoted or sent. Your customer and business data is used only to provide the assistance you switch on.
